Sprasa HR – Full Product Demo | HR & Payroll Software for Nepali Organisations

DESCRIPTION
Sprasa HR keeps employee records, attendance, leave, payroll and reports in one place — built for Nepali organisations (NPR, Asia/Kathmandu time, Shrawan fiscal year, SSF / PF / CIT).

In this demo:
✔ HR dashboard – today's attendance, leave and payroll at a glance
✔ People – employee profiles with PAN, SSF and documents, plus an organisation chart
✔ Attendance – employees check in from the browser, HR approves each check-in
✔ Leave – apply online, balance checked instantly, manager and HR approval, team leave calendar
✔ Payroll – monthly runs (Draft → Reviewed → Approved → Paid), tax, SSF and PF worked out, PDF payslips
✔ Reports – 21 reports, export to Excel or PDF
